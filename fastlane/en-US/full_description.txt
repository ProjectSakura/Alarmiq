Alarmiq is a modern, challenge-based alarm clock that ensures you are fully awake before you can dismiss your alarm.

Features:
- Multiple difficulty levels: None, Normal, Hard, Nightmare, and Punishment.
- Diverse challenges: Math problems, reverse typing, and paragraph transcription.
- Full lockdown mode: Prevent alarm bypass with screen pinning and aggressive re-launching.
- World Clock: Stay updated with localized times for over 400 cities globally, including extensive support for Indian cities.
- Stopwatch and Timer: Built-in productivity tools.
- Open Source and Free: No ads, no tracking, and fully open-source.

Stop snoozing your life away and wake up the right way with Alarmiq!
